Market 2026-08-18

Beyond Market Consensus: How to Find Overlooked Investment Opportunities

When market consensus concentrates on one direction, how do you systematically examine the fundamental changes consensus has obscured? Four cases (energy storage, non-ferrous metals, innovative pharma, and non-bank financials) build a cross-dimensional cross-validation research framework.

Research 2026-08-12

Case: AI Supply Chain — Is the Supply-Driven Cycle Over?

Applying the Three-Layer Signal framework to the AI industry: supply signals remain tight, capital signals are accelerating expansion, and earnings signals are improving but not yet mature. Currently in the transition from investment phase to delivery phase.

Learning 2026-07-30

Behavioral Biases in Investing: Why Good Analysis Can Still Fail

Frameworks tell you what to look at, but they cannot guarantee that what you see is real. Three cognitive biases (availability bias, confirmation bias, and herding) can distort the input, execution, and output of analytical frameworks.

Research 2026-07-23

How to Analyze Technology Revolutions: An AI Investment Framework

From supply constraints to commercial deployment: building a research framework for studying technology revolutions, using the AI industry as a case study. Find supply constraints, observe capital investment, wait for earnings delivery.

Market 2026-07-16

K-Shaped Divergence in Markets: Three Forces Reshaping the Economy

K-shaped divergence is not a random fluctuation. It is the result of three long-term economic forces working together: supply constraints in the AI industry, transformation of the macro policy framework, and restructuring of the global industrial division of labor.

Learning 2026-07-13

Historical Stock Market Patterns: When They Stop Working

What changes is statistical regularity; what remains constant is the logic of earnings-driven patterns. AI supply rigidity, misaligned capital structure, and policy framework shifts have together triggered a fundamental restructuring of the market.

The goal is not to predict markets, but to understand them.
